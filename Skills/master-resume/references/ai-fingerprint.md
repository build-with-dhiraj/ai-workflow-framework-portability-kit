# AI-Fingerprint Avoidance

Secondary safety net (the primary defense is specific real facts + JD vocabulary + named entities). Run the **12-item scan** on every document before presenting. Cover letters are most vulnerable — apply with extra weight there.

## 1. Banned words

**Tier 1 — dead giveaways (never use):** delve, tapestry, multifaceted, pivotal, realm, synergy, paradigm, holistic, nuanced, foster, embark, leverage (verb), utilize, harness, spearhead, cornerstone, landscape (metaphorical), journey (metaphorical), cutting-edge, novel (unless quoting the JD), groundbreaking.

**Adjectives → replacement:** robust→strong/reliable · comprehensive→thorough/broad · innovative→new/original/omit · pivotal→key/central · meticulous→careful/precise · diverse→varied/wide-ranging · extensive→broad/deep/"10+ years of".

**Verbs → replacement:** leverage→use/apply/draw on · utilize→use · harness→apply/use · spearhead→lead/start/launch · foster→support/build/grow · facilitate→run/lead/coordinate/enable · showcase→show/demonstrate · underscore→show/highlight · bolster→strengthen/support.

**Adverbs (avoid):** meticulously, notably, subsequently (use "then"/"later"), remarkably, seamlessly, thereby.

**Metaphorical nouns (avoid):** tapestry, landscape, journey, realm, synergy, paradigm, cornerstone.

*Technical exceptions:* "landscape" literal (free-energy landscape, threat landscape) is fine; "novel" verbatim from a JD is fine. Judge by context.

## 2. Banned phrases
Openers/transitions: "In today's rapidly evolving…", "At the forefront of…", "It is worth noting that…", "In an era of…", "This experience has taught me…", "I am uniquely positioned to…".
Resume/CL: "proven track record", "passionate about", "I am excited to apply", "demonstrated ability to", "strong foundation in", "well-versed in", "adept at".
Academic: "groundbreaking research", "cutting-edge methodology", "novel approach" (say what's new), "significant contributions to the field", "at the intersection of X and Y" (name the specific intersection).

## 3. Structural rules
- **No "It's not X — it's Y"** constructions.
- **No rhetorical Q+A** ("What makes this unique? …").
- **No 3+ stacked "-ing" phrases.**
- **#1 structural marker — no "-ing" analysis endings on bullets.** Bullets must end on a concrete result/metric/object. ✗ "…contributing to improved efficiency" · ✓ "…contributing to a 15% reduction."
- **Max 2 em-dashes (`---`) per document.** Replace extras with commas/semicolons/parentheses. Honors/Fellowships items use `. ` not `---`.
- **Vary sentence length** — mix 8–12 word and 20–30 word sentences; 3 consecutive same-length sentences flag as AI.
- **No same-structure paragraph starts** ("My research…", "My experience…", "My approach…").
- **≤2 "X, Y, and Z" triplets** per document.

## 4. Positive human markers (aim for these)
Specific numbers ("ran 847 MD simulations" not "extensive simulations") · front-loaded specifics · named entities (tools, methods, journals, institutions) · the JD's own jargon · short connectors (so/but/and/then, not consequently/however/additionally) · first-person specificity in CLs ("I built") · inside knowledge (group/facility/program names) · deliberate sentence-length variety · occasional And/But openers in CLs (1–2/page) · contractions in industry CLs · one concrete human detail per CL page.

## 5. The 12-item post-generation scan (mandatory)
1. [ ] Any Tier-1 banned word?
2. [ ] Any banned phrase (§2)?
3. [ ] More than 2 `---` in the document?
4. [ ] Any bullet ending in an "-ing" analysis phrase?
5. [ ] 3+ consecutive similar-length sentences?
6. [ ] Repeated paragraph-start structure?
7. [ ] More than 2 "X, Y, and Z" triplets?
8. [ ] CL opens generic instead of company-specific?
9. [ ] Metaphorical "landscape/journey/realm/tapestry"?
10. [ ] Passive voice in >20% of bullet verbs?
11. [ ] Honors/Fellowships using `---` instead of `. `?
12. [ ] Any banned adverb (meticulously, notably, subsequently…)?

**Any failure → fix before presenting.** These are detectable patterns, not optional polish.
